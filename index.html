A FEW FAIR QUESTIONS

Before we talk.

Can you guarantee leads or closed projects?+

No. Results depend on your market, budget, offer and campaign performance. Project closures also depend on your response, consultation and sales process. We agree what to track and review performance honestly.

What if advertising brings unsuitable enquiries?+

We use locations, messaging and enquiry questions to communicate who your service suits. Unsuitable enquiries can still happen. Reviewing lead quality together helps guide adjustments.

Do I need a website to work with you?+

No. We assess your current setup first. If you do not have a website and need one, I can build it as part of an agreed scope. Every service is not compulsory.

How is the success fee tracked?+

Before starting, we agree which channels and leads qualify, how existing contacts are excluded, how you share closure updates, and when payment is due. The success fee and retainer are discussed for your scope.

How much experience do you have?+

I’m building my portfolio. You can explore the Leafy Studios website as an example of my work. I do not present it as a proven advertising or sales case study.
